ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3)EU
Consent required for non-essential cookies
Directive 2002/58/EC requires prior consent before storing or reading non-essential trackers on a user's device (transposed in France by Art. 82 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés).
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj
CNIL Délibérations 2020-091 / 2020-092FR
Refusing cookies must be as easy as accepting them
The CNIL guidelines and recommendation on cookies require that refusing be as simple as accepting. In January 2022 the CNIL fined Google €150M and Facebook €60M because refusing took more clicks than accepting.
https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies/lignes-directrices-modificatives-et-recommandation
GDPR Art. 7(3)EU
Withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 requires that it be as easy to withdraw consent as to give it.
