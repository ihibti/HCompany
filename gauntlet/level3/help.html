Help Center

Find answers about your account, billing and watchlist.

How do I change my plan?
Go to your Account page and choose "Manage plan" to upgrade or downgrade between Basic, Standard and Premium.
How do I update my payment method?
Open Account → Payment methods and add a new card. Your billing date stays the same.
Billing & membership
Your subscription renews automatically each month. To review charges, open Account → Billing history.

Looking to make changes to your membership? If you wish to end your membership, our retention team would love to help — click here to see how.
How do I reset my password?
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to you within a few minutes.
Can I download shows to watch offline?
Yes — Premium includes downloads on up to 2 devices.
